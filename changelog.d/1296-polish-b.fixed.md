In an open conversation of people and agents, an edit or a delete another console made in a page of earlier messages you had loaded now shows without reopening it, as it does in a channel. A new message no longer leaves the oldest message of the newest page out of the list while earlier pages are loaded, and a conversation that was cleared no longer keeps the old "Earlier messages" button. In a channel, a Direct Message or a conversation, Earlier messages no longer loses the page it loaded, or the result of a read for a signal, when the two overlap, and a page that was still loading when you opened another thread no longer lands under the new one, even when you come back to the first. Each of the two waits for the other for 1.5 seconds at most, and the wait starts over when you open another thread, so an earlier page or a read that is slow or never answers no longer stops another thread from loading, or leaves Earlier messages disabled. While an earlier page is still loading, a live update of the same thread can be held back for up to those 1.5 seconds, while a normal page loads in a few tens of milliseconds. If a page takes longer than that while a new message arrives, that message can be missing until the next signal or a reload. An answer that throws while the page's reads land no longer stops the answers behind it from landing (#1296).

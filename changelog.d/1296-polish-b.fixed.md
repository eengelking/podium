In an open conversation of people and agents, an edit or a delete another console made in a page of earlier messages you had loaded now shows without reopening it, as it does in a channel. A new message no longer leaves the oldest message of the newest page out of the list while earlier pages are loaded, and a conversation that was cleared no longer keeps the old "Earlier messages" button. In a channel, a Direct Message or a conversation, Earlier messages no longer loses the page it loaded, or the result of a read for a signal, when the two overlap, and a page that was still loading when you opened another thread no longer lands under the new one. An answer that throws while the page's reads land no longer stops the answers behind it from landing (#1296).

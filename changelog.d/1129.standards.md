`EdgeTab` gains a placement, `opts` (`.edge-tab-opts`): the same 32 x 24 pill in the banner's row instead of on an edge, with its tip lined up on the side it is on. Shut it draws a sliders glyph, the one EdgeTab without a chevron; open it draws a chevron pointing right. A new component, Wall options (`components/WallOptions.tsx`, `.wallopts`, `.wallopts-body`), in the Top bar section: that pill plus Refresh every, Shortcuts, Theme and the mode radios in a drawer on `--surface` with a `--border` outline, the only way a page shows them. It replaces `.bannerctl`, which is removed. Approved by the maintainer with the Wall options mockup (#1129).

An Admin's pause, resume or removal of a person's messaging links, and an Admin's edit or removal of a public channel someone else opened, are now kept owed to that person until they have seen them. Before, only the person's live reporter sessions were told, in memory and for 5 minutes, so a person with no live session at that moment, or whose server restarted in those minutes, was never told. Now a session of theirs that reports later shows the same words once, and the Chat overview shows a quiet line. The Admin log's rows for a channel edit or removal name the person who opened it and how many sessions were told, as the link stops do (#1328).

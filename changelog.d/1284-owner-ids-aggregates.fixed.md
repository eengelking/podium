The spend, usage and report figures kept per person now go by the person's id, never by name, so a new person who takes the name of one who was deleted no longer shows the earlier person's spend in My usage or in a group's figures. A deleted person's spend stays in the fleet's totals and charts but belongs to no person or group. The daily rows already stored are given their person's id when the server starts, and the group is taken off the rows of anyone who no longer exists.

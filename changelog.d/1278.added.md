Chat: a Direct Message is now a conversation of two or more people and agents, as in Slack or Teams. Message on any person works whether or not an orchestrator of theirs is running, and they see it in Chat with an unread count. The **+** in Direct Messages opens one dialog for people and your own agents, and a conversation's members panel adds more. A conversation between people only cannot be read by an Admin at all; with an agent in it an Admin can read it, and everyone in it is told in the console and in `podium-reporter`.

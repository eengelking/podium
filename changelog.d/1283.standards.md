A wall notice that reports an event, not a standing state, is closed one at a time: its X dismisses only that row and the confirmation is titled "Notice dismissed", as the update notice's X hides one release (#499); the notice's on/off switch under Admin > Settings > Wall notices turns the whole kind off. The first such notice is "Concluded direct messages". The maintainer approved this departure from "closing turns it off for everyone" in the mockup `mockup/podium-chat-dm-ended.html` (#1283).

Chat's sidebar has capital 12 px section titles that stay at the top while you scroll and fold with a chevron, a count in each title, and a hairline between sections. New channel and New direct message are now a + in the title with a tip (Create a channel, Start a direct message); at the 50-channel limit the Channels + is off and says why. A conversation shows its agent's tile or both parties overlapped, a row with new messages has a bold name, the selected row has a bar on its left, and a row's menu opens under the row and flips up when there is no room. People and agents also lists the other people; clicking one, or choosing Message in its menu, opens New direct message with their name searched.

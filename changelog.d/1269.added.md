In Chat you can edit and delete your own messages, in My group, General, a public channel and a Direct Message. Right-click a message of yours (a long press on a touch screen, or Enter, the menu key or Shift+F10 on it) for Edit message and Delete message. Edit puts the text in a box with Save and Cancel and shows "edited" with the time; Delete asks first, then leaves a line saying who deleted it and when, and the text is gone from the server. Once a message has been sent to an agent (waiting for it, being worked on or done; for a Direct Message, queued, handed or delivered, or approved by its owner) it is locked: the menu stays but its items are off, with the reason under them, and the server refuses the change too. A message that was not sent to an agent (not delivered, held, waiting for another person's approval, or with no @) can still be changed, and an edit never sends anything again, so it cannot add or remove an @. From the keyboard, Up in an empty composer goes to your latest message, Up and Down move between your own messages, E edits, Delete or Backspace deletes and Escape goes back one step. Edits and deletes reach every open console without a refresh, `podium_chat_read` shows an agent the edited text and only a note for a deleted message, and a message is yours by your person id, not by your name. An Admin gets no way to edit or delete anyone else's message. (#1269)

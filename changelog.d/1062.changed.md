A reporter's report no longer reads the Admin readings from the store on every report, or looks a deleted Direct Message up again each time. The readings are kept for two seconds across every session's reports and dropped at once when an Admin read or stop is noted, a conversation with no subject is remembered for ten seconds, and what is remembered is pruned after the five-minute window. A session that starts after a reading began, and a restart, still get the callout, and what it says is unchanged (#1062).

`samples` has an index on its time column in SQLite, PostgreSQL and MariaDB. The tick's token-rate query no longer reads the whole table (a day of samples from 20 sessions: 561 ms to 9 ms on MariaDB, 35 ms to 2 ms on SQLite) and the hourly retention delete is faster (845 ms to 163 ms on MariaDB). The index takes about 12 to 41 MB at that size and makes a sample write slightly slower on SQLite. A database gets the index when the server starts after the upgrade (#755).

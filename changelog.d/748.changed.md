The event stream ends a client that has stopped reading once more than 8 MB is queued for it, and logs `stream.slow_client_ended` once with the reason. Before, the server held every message for such a client without limit, about 70 MB a minute at the large fleet; now they are ended in about 8 s. The browser reconnects after 2 s and receives a fresh snapshot, and a client that is reading is never ended (#748).

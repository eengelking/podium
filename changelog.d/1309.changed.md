Chat sends fewer statements to the database, and the number no longer grows with the people or the conversations. A post to General or a public channel counts for everyone in two statements instead of two for each person, the list of a person's conversations is three statements instead of three for each one, the Chat overview reads only the person's own messages and their rights and limits in a few statements instead of nine for each conversation, and a session that leaves looks for its held mentions in two statements instead of one for each public channel. This is the same in SQLite, PostgreSQL and MariaDB, and what Chat shows is unchanged (#1309).

Fourteen Admin > Reporting sections now follow the panel rule set on Harness updates: the summary figures and the chart after them are one panel, and each table with column headers is a panel of its own under the same heading. They are Usage by day; Projection, Spend over time, and Tokens and cache hit rate on Spend; People and groups; Sessions by day, Length and waiting, Agents launched, and Context fullness at the end on Sessions; Cost per tool call and per session, Tool failures, Permission prompts, and Harness versions on Tools; and Failures by day. Nothing else in them changes. The samples a session list reads for many sessions are now ordered by second and then by session, so the order is the same on every database (#1136).

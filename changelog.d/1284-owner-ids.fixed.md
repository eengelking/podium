A session and a reporter now belong to a person by that person's id, never by name, so a new person who takes the name of one who was deleted no longer inherits the earlier person's sessions or reporters: they are left to nobody (the Admin can still read and delete them), and "My sessions", answering a session, My reporters and the checks on a person's own sessions all go by the id. Sessions and reporters already stored are given their person's id when the server starts, and one whose person no longer exists is left to nobody.

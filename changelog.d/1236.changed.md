Chat's thread header shows its members as an avatar stack with a count, which opens a panel on the right (a sheet over the thread on a narrow window) with a search, filters, People and Agents and "Showing N of M". It replaces the row of participant chips; a Direct Message names its parties with avatars before the title and has no stack or panel. The list is built from what the page already has, so a public channel's list is only the people and agents the page knows, and the panel says so (#1236).

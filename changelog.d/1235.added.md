Chat: right-click an avatar in a thread, or press Enter, the ContextMenu key or Shift+F10 on a message, for a menu with Message and Mention for a person, and Message, Mention and Open orchestration page for an agent. Message is on every person and agent in the thread, the members panel and the sidebar, and opens New direct message with their name searched and their one orchestrator chosen. Mention writes their address at the caret in the channel's message box. A Direct Message has no Mention, and an Admin reading gets no item that sends (#1235).

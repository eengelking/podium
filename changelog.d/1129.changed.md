The display controls under the top bar (Refresh every, Shortcuts, Theme and Light / Dark / System) are folded into a Wall options drawer at the right of the banner. It starts shut as one button; click it to open the drawer, and close it with the same button, a click outside or Escape. Every page that shows the banner (Live fleet, Board, My reporters, Traffic, Chat, History and Admin) draws the same drawer with all four controls, and a setting changed on one page shows on every other: History and Admin now show and save Refresh every, and Admin shows the Shortcuts switch (#1129).

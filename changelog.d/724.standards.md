A pattern for how a report section is split into panels: a section is one titled heading over one or more `.panel` objects, and what goes in a panel follows whether the content has headers of its own. A summary row (`div.tool`, such as the big figures) stays in the same panel as the content after it when that content has no headers of its own (a chart, with the rule under the summary), and the figures and a chart are one object. When the content after it has headers of its own (a table with column headers), it is a separate `.panel` under the same section heading, spaced by the section's own gap (12px), and a table that follows a chart gets its own panel too. Seen on Admin > Reporting > Tools ("Tool calls by day": summary and chart in one panel) and Admin > Reporting > Harness updates ("Features that stopped": summary panel, then table panel). Approved by the maintainer with the Harness updates mockup (#724).

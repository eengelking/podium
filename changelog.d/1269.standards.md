Adds the Chat message menu and the editor, the delete confirmation and the deleted-message line to the standards: a menu that opens at the pointer with no button on the row, in the existing menu's 13px type, with 11px key hints (`ed-kbd`) and the reason a message is locked under its disabled items; an in-place editor (`ed-box`) as wide as `min(100%, 560px)`, right-aligned like the person's own messages and full width at 400px, with one focus ring; an "edited" time after the text (`ed-edited`); a dashed-border line with a trash icon for a deleted message (`ed-del`); and a delete confirmation that names who loses the message, with Cancel focused first, arrow keys between its two buttons and a red Delete message button. The maintainer approved all of it in the mockup `mockup/podium-chat-edit-delete.html` (#1269).

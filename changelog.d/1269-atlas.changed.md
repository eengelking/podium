The atlas describes editing and deleting your own Chat messages: the four `PATCH` and `DELETE` routes under `/api/fleet/respond/chat`, the lock rule the store applies (a channel message once an agent mention is waiting, working or done, a Direct Message once it is queued, handed or delivered, or approved by its owner), the `edited_at`, `deleted_at` and `author_id` columns (SQLite migration 46, PostgreSQL and MariaDB step 22), the tombstone, the `changeAcross` bridge to a copy that waits for approval, the new Admin log kinds, what an agent reads, and the message menu in the console (#1269).

Chat and Direct Message data is stored once, with nothing lost: the unread counts keep one row for each person and thread that has something unread (a thread read to the end keeps none), the group messages, channels, Direct Messages and conversations drop the columns and indexes nothing read, and a message's `updatedAt` is worked out from its other times. The stored last-read cursor is removed, with the maintainer's approval: no route ever returned it, and `POST /chat/read` still checks `upTo` and then ignores it. The database upgrades on its own (SQLite migrations 54 and 55, PostgreSQL and MariaDB steps 30 and 31); every count that is not zero is kept (#1296)

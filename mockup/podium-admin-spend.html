Spend as its own Admin page

Mockup for #239, second direction: the panel leaves the fleet page's right-hand Activity panel entirely. Spend gets its own entry in Admin's side menu, under Activity, and its own page. The frame is Admin at its real layout, with Podium's colors. Everything is live: 3 or 64 people, person or group, chart or table, rows per page, theme and width.

What this assumes

  • Menu: a "Spend" button in the Activity group, above Admin log, with the number of people (or groups) on it, like People and Groups have. Narrow screens turn the menu into the top tab strip, as every Admin page does.
  • Page: the standard Admin pattern: header and lead, then one section with a toolbar (Group by, Show as), a panel, and the existing Admin pager (Showing 1 to 25 of 64, Rows per page, Previous, Page 1 of 3, Next).
  • Caveat: "Estimated from tokens, not a bill." is now in the page's lead, so nothing hides in a tooltip. The "Each bar is one person…" line is gone.
  • Total: the amount spent and the live sessions sit in the upper right of the panel, on the toolbar row, so the bars start right under it. They drop under the controls on narrow screens. They count everyone and do not change with the page or the page size, and the table's Total row matches it.
  • Paging: 25, 50 or 100 rows per page, Admin's own sizes. Rows are largest first on every page, so page 2 continues where page 1 stops.
  • Fleet page: the "Spend today, by person" panel is removed from the right-hand Activity panel, and from any other page that shows it. This page is the only home for it, so the fleet page's Activity list moves up to the top of the right panel.
  • Who sees it: only Administrators, because Admin is Administrators-only (#215). People who are not Administrators no longer see per-person spend anywhere.

Questions for you

  • Today only, as the panel is now, or also a date range (7 days, 30 days)? A range needs the server to keep per-person spend by day, which it does not do today. I drew today only.
  • The fleet page's people bands still show each person's share of today's spend. Those stay, since they are on the wall and not in the panel. Is that what you want?

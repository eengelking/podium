Where the theme picker goes

Three ways to put the picker in the row under the header, instead of in the header. In all three the credits move to the bottom left corner of the left rail, with a copyright line beside them. The wording of the footer is a first draft.

A. In the info row

The row that says "Live fleet. Cost is an estimate..." keeps its text on the left and gets the full picker on the right. No new row, so the header loses its crowding at no cost in height. The row is already hidden by Hide top bar and wall mode, so the picker disappears with them.

B. Its own slim bar

A second bar of its own under the header, for "view" controls: the picker on the left, the cost note pushed to the right. Room to add other display settings later (density, wall mode). Costs about 34 px of height, and it needs its own collapse behavior (it would go with Hide top bar).

C. One chip that opens a small menu

The info row ends with a single chip showing the current theme and mode with a swatch strip. Clicking it opens a panel holding the dropdown and the mode buttons. Quietest in the row and the best fit at phone width, but it is one click deeper than A or B. Shown open below.

The credits footer in the bottom left corner

It lives at the foot of the left rail, so it follows the rail: two lines, small and muted. The first line links to the credits. The second carries the copyright, license and version. When the left pane is collapsed the footer would collapse with it, so there are two ways to keep the credits reachable.

The context bar goes red at 80%

From 75% to 79% the bar stays amber, as it is today. At 80% and above it is red, the same red as the blowout level 2 badge, and never both colors at once. Red also carries a bright outline and a solid cap at the fill's end, so it reads in grayscale. The percentage stays next to the bar, and a screen reader hears "nearly full" after the number.

Rules

Amber below 80% keeps its meaning (the window is filling). Red at 80% and above wins over the one-blowout amber fill. Two blowouts keep the red stripes. On the card, where the bar is 3px tall, the outline is dropped and only the cap stays. Applies wherever ContextBar shows: the card, the orchestrator page, the agent tree and rows, and both detail panels, in every theme.

Agent activity feed

The right-hand panel is the agent panel from the orchestrator screen. The new Activity section sits under the Report buttons. It lists the agent's recent tool calls and the text it writes between them, newest at the bottom, and it fills while the agent works. The reporter option activityFeed is off by default; with it off, the section says what it would show and how to turn it on.

The agent panel

Running: the agent does something every couple of seconds, but the panel fetches only on the interval you pick (5s to start; 10, 20, 30 or 60s), so new entries arrive in batches, and only while this panel is open and the tab is visible. Close the panel and nothing is fetched. The list follows the newest entry. Scroll up to read earlier entries and it stops following; "Jump to latest" brings it back. Opening the panel starts the feed; it begins empty and fills from that moment, and leaving clears it, so coming back starts again from then. Finished (here as an agent you were watching as it ended): the entries stop and the final answer is one click away in Report, as today.

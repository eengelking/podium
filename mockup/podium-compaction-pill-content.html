Where the 80% line falls

The reporter compares the tokens in the context before a compaction with the model's window. Below 80% it logs a compact event. At 80% or more it counts a blowout, as before. 80% is also where the context bar already turns red.

Auto compaction, logged as a compact eventWindow exceeded, a blowout80% or more, counted
  • Fill not knownCounted as a blowout. The reporter cannot tell, so the old behavior stays.
  • Stop, then compactionA window-exceeded stop and the auto compaction within 2 seconds after it are one blowout, at any fill.
  • Manual and pluginStill compact events, never blowouts.

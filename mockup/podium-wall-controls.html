Wall controls, one row

Mockup for #245 and #246. With two or more people reporting, Group by, Order by, You are and Only me move into the wall's existing control row. Example data; the names are made up. Every frame is a live copy of the same markup at the toolbar's own width, so the wrapping you see is the wrapping the page will do. Click the buttons to see the pressed state.

  1. Order by and Sort by. Both stay, because they order different things. Order by sits inside the outlined People cluster and orders the bands; Sort by sits with the filter and orders the cards inside each band. Their tooltips say so.
  2. #246. Group by reads People, Group, All and, when someone is signed in as a person, Me. "All" replaces "None" and saves about 50 px; it sits near the status filter's own "All", so check it reads clearly.
  3. "Showing X" and Show everyone are gone. The left list already narrows the wall to a person or group when you click its header, and "All orchestrators" undoes it, so the wall's own row no longer repeats that.
  4. You are and Only me are gone. Who "me" is comes from the browser's sign-in (GET /respond/me). Me shows a flat wall of just the signed-in person's orchestrators, and Order by is hidden for it, as for All. The "you" tag, your band first and "your group" follow the sign-in. Signed out, or signed in as Admin, there is no Me and no "you" marking (see the signed-out frame).
  5. Give-way order, each step measured in the frames below: the caption "9 shown · 14 cleared today" becomes an info icon with the same words as its tooltip (under about 1390 px of toolbar); the People cluster takes its own line under the filter row (under about 1225 px); its "People" label goes (under 640 px); the buttons tighten (under 585 px). The text filter grows to 260 px when there is room and gives that back down to 110 px before anything wraps. Nothing is hidden or moved into a menu.
  6. One row needs about 1390 px of toolbar for everything in full, or about 1225 px with the caption as an icon. That is a 1400 px window with both panels collapsed (1320 px) or a 1900 px window with them open (1300 px).
  7. Assumed widths. Left list 260 px and right panel 340 px open, about 40 px each collapsed, and the right panel hidden at 1100 px and below as in the people mockup. Check these against the app before the build.
  8. No people reporting. The People cluster is not drawn and the row is exactly today's: filter, Sort by, text filter, caption.

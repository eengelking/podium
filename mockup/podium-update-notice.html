What the rules say

It follows the wall-notice rules: bottom of the wall, an X, a confirmation that points to Admin > Settings, and an on/off switch there. It differs in one way: the X does not turn the check off. It hides this release for everyone, and the notice comes back when a newer release is published. The switch is what stops the check.

A failed check (offline, rate limited, no answer) shows nothing at all, not even an error, and the next daily check tries again. The notice never shows when the versions match or this server is the newer one.

Podium orchestrator page

The page keeps its layout and every fact. What changes is how it is put together, so it matches the fleet card: the header, one status band and the facts become one surface of fixed layers, and the stack of callouts above the facts goes away.

The problem, and the fix

Drawn from the code, not a screenshot. Today up to five callouts can stack between the header and the facts, each in its own place and color, and the page says nothing about the work until they end. In the proposal the state is one band, always the last thing on the summary, the same height and in the same place, as on the card.

Today

Callouts appear on their own conditions and push the facts and the work down.

Proposed

One surface: header, facts, then the band at the bottom. The callouts become the band and two cells in the facts grid.

Anatomy of the page

Read top to bottom: who and what it is doing, what it costs and how healthy it is, then the verdict in a band at the bottom, then the work itself. This is the card’s order. Hairlines separate the layers, as on the card.

What is this, and what is it doing?The state shape and name, then the plan title and current step, folder, session and git line. The three charts stay at the right. The status word leaves this line; the shape beside the name stays.
What is it costing, and how healthy is it?Labelled readouts in fixed cells. Blowouts and Reporter are now cells here, so the blowout and reporter callouts are gone. The blowout sentence opens between the header and this row, where an alert sits.
Does it need me?The status band, always last, as on the card. It replaces the waiting, background wait and heartbeat-lost callouts. Same height every time, with room for one detail line. Calm states are a light tint; waiting and heartbeat lost are heavier.
The workThe plan and the agent tree, side by side as today, with the card’s surface and hairlines. Their content does not change.

Every state

The top of the page changes between states, so most specimens show the summary only. The last four also show the plan and the agent tree. Shape carries the state as well as color: the shape by the name and in the band differs for each state.

Narrow width check

A 400 px page, as in the stacked layout below 1040 px. The charts wrap under the name, the facts become two columns, and the plan and tree stack. The Remove button moves under the text when there is no room beside it, and keeps one place.

Where every fact goes

Nothing is dropped. The changes are the callouts, the status chip, and two new cells.

Kanban board

Mockup for issue #346. A new page, reached from the top bar next to History at #/board. It shows every active orchestrator's plan steps by state, read-only. Clicking a card opens that orchestrator's page. Use the controls to see each state and variant; the settings you pick change the specimens below.

What this mockup proposes for the open questions

Columns (decided)Pending, In progress, Review, Blocked, Done. Pending takes Backlog's place. Review exists for review logic to come; until then it holds in-progress steps whose session waits on its person (a question, a plan or a permission prompt), and nothing else moves in. Blocked keeps its own column, since Claude Code reports it.
Pinned strip (#359)The "no open steps" strip is pinned to the bottom of the page, directly above the legend, 16px from the page edges and from the legend, so it reads as a panel and not as part of the footer. It overlays the board and does not move as cards come and go: try Short board, 1 in strip. The board's scroll area ends with bottom padding equal to the strip's height (measured, so it follows the strip as it changes), so the last card in the tallest column can be scrolled clear of it; try Large fleet and scroll to the end. A focused card scrolls into view clear of the strip. With nothing in the strip, nothing is pinned and the board has the full height (None in strip).
Long strip and the (i) note (#359)The strip is at most 200px tall or 42% of the page, whichever is less. Its header stays; the chips wrap and scroll inside it past that (Many in strip), so it cannot cover most of the board. The (i) note opens as a popover above the strip, anchored to it, so it never grows the strip and is not clipped by the legend; it closes with the button again or Escape, which returns focus to the button. The strip keeps its label and its place in the tab order (after the columns).
Narrow layout (#359)On a phone the console stacks and the page scrolls as a whole with the legend at its end. There the strip is sticky to the bottom of the viewport, above the legend, with the same padding, and the board ends with the strip's height of room so it never hides the last card. This mockup's narrow frame is a fixed-height viewport, so it shows the same pinned strip.
Which sessions count (decided)Every session on the Fleet page is on the board: running, waiting, delegating, stale or ended. One with open steps fills columns. One with no plan, or with every step done, is listed in the strip under the board and still links to its page. A stale session's open steps show in their columns like any other.
Done column (decided)A done step stays until its orchestrator is finished, then its Done cards fall off. Finished means every step is done and the session is idle, stale or ended. A running session with every step done keeps its Done cards, with an "all done" mark on its lane. No new signal is sent.
Running counts (#409)The first two of the tiles under the Board header (#426 adds two more, below): Orchestrators running and Subagents running. They are the Fleet page's own KPI tiles (same classes, same label for subagents), so no new component. Both read the same two numbers the server already derives for the Fleet page (Kpis.running and Kpis.agentsRunning), and nothing new is sent or stored. The Board does not count on its own, so the two pages cannot disagree. The tile rules are the Fleet page's, unchanged, so the tiles wrap as the Fleet strip does, and the captions wrap and are never clipped. A number changing width does not move the columns, which sit below the strip. No flash or other animation: the number just changes. Proposed for the maintainer: the tile label \"Orchestrators running\" and the two captions are new wording on the existing tile (Fleet's first tile reads \"Orchestrators live\" with the running/waiting/stale split as its caption). If you would rather keep Fleet's exact tile, say so and the Board shows \"Orchestrators live\" instead.
What counts as running (#409)Counted: an orchestrator whose status is running, and a subagent whose status is running (forks included). Not counted: an orchestrator waiting on its person, idle on background work, stale or ended; its subagents drop out once it ends. The caption under the orchestrator count says "of N live, M waiting", so a waiting session is visible without being counted. N is the server's Kpis.live (every session not yet dropped), and M is the server's waiting count, which leaves out a session idle on background work (#119); the page takes both from the server and never recounts. This specimen has no idle state to show. Fleet shows the running figure only as text in its first tile's caption; here it is a tile of its own. Try Simulate update 3 and 4: docs-sweep starts running (orchestrators +1), release-prep ends (orchestrators −1, subagents −1).
Me and layout (#409, picked)The counts always show the whole fleet. Mixed or By orchestrator, the Group filter and Me change which cards show, never the counts, so they equal the Fleet page's. While Me or a group is on, the caption under Subagents reads "whole fleet" and the tile group's name says so for screen readers.
Plans | All switch (#426, for approval)A two-state switch, Plans | All, at the upper left of the Board header, right after the title. It is the page's own segmented control (the Board's kb-seg in the app, which also holds Mixed | By orchestrator), so no new component: two real buttons with aria-pressed, the same focus ring, reachable by Tab and operated with Enter or Space. Plans shows only orchestrators that have open steps, in the columns: today's page without the strip. All is today's page: the columns, plus the strip of sessions with no plan, an empty plan, or only done steps. The choice is kept in localStorage under podium.board with the Board's other preferences (a new view field) and survives a reload. After a press the focus stays on the pressed button, and the group is named "Sessions shown" for screen readers. The switch changes nothing in the tiles, the left list, or the Me, Group and Mixed | By orchestrator controls, which work in both modes. Try Plans and All on Busy fleet, None in strip, No plans at all and Waiting, no plan.
Plan figures move into the tile row (#426)The sentence "5 orchestrators with open steps, 19 steps" is gone from the header. Its numbers are two more tiles in the same row, the Fleet page's own tile classes, so no new component. All four tiles are whole-fleet facts, so the switch, Me and Group change none of them, as the issue requires: Orchestrators with plans (5, "of 8 live", so the 3 sessions All adds in the strip are visible as the difference) and Plan steps (19, "6 done"). The old sentence said "open steps" but counted Done cards too; the tile says what it counts. While Me or a group is on, both captions read "whole fleet", as the Subagents tile does. Both numbers are worked out from the sessions the page already receives, so nothing new is sent or stored. Tiles are a fixed size, so nothing in the header or under it moves. On a narrow board the row is 2 by 2. With nothing to put in a column, the empty state in Plans reads "None of the 8 active sessions has open steps. Switch to All to list them."; in All it keeps today's wording. The labels and captions are new wording for the maintainer to approve.
Open questions, with a proposal to pick from (#426)Default: All, so nothing changes for anyone until they choose. A plan whose steps are all done: counts as having none (it is in the strip today, so only in All). A session waiting on its person with no plan: counts as having a plan, because it has a card in Review; it shows there in both modes and never in the strip (Waiting, no plan: api-explore). Not changed: the card design, the columns, the Fleet page, which sessions the Board receives, and the Me and Group filters. If you would rather have Plans as the default, or the strip hidden by Plans instead of removed, say so.
Mixed or groupedMixed by default, so the board answers "what is in progress across everything". A toggle groups each column by orchestrator, with a lane label. The orchestrator name and model chip sit on every card in mixed view and drop off in grouped view.
LimitsEach column scrolls inside itself and pages at 25 cards, with the same footer as the Files and Plan tabs ("Showing 1–25 of 40" and page buttons) at the bottom of the column. Empty columns say what they are waiting for. A 100-step plan fills one lane, never the page. Each column is at least 210px wide, so a board narrower than five of them scrolls sideways inside its own area; on a phone it becomes tabs.
Left and right panels (decided from the running app)The board is a page of the console, so it keeps the same top bar, banner, orchestrator list on the left and activity panel on the right, with the same edge tabs, [ and ] keys and \ for the top bar. The left list, activity panel and legend are the same components as on the fleet page, not look-alikes: the list keeps its model chips, plan bars, agent counts, status dots and footer, and the activity panel its newest-first list. The board opens with both panels collapsed to their 24px edge tabs, so the five columns get the full width; open either from its tab. The board remembers its own panel state, separate from the fleet page's, so opening a panel here does not change the fleet page. A click on a row in the list still opens that orchestrator, and the activity panel shows the same events as on the fleet page. The top bar gets a Fleet button, which goes back to the fleet page, and a Board button, beside History; the page pill reads Board.
FiltersGroup, person and a "Me" toggle, the same ones the wall has, with "Me" kept in localStorage. "Me" appears only when someone is signed in; signed out, the chip is not shown. Toggle the signed-in state above the console to see both.
UpdatesUse Simulate update above the console. A card whose step changes slides to its new column in 200 ms and flashes for a couple of seconds. A new step fades in. When an orchestrator finishes, its Done cards fade out and it moves to the strip. Each change also adds a line to the activity panel and is announced to screen readers. Reduced motion keeps the flash and drops the slide and fades.
CardsOne link per card; its accessible name reads step, state and orchestrator. An in-progress card shows activeText with a slow pulse that stops under reduced motion. Nothing drags.

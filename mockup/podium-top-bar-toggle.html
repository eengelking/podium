Podium Top Bar Toggle (#402) Mode Theme Page Click a tab or press \ to toggle every bar below.

A tab in the middle, not a text button

The text button Hide top bar leaves the bar's button row. In its place, a small tab with a chevron sits in the middle of the bar's bottom edge. Hidden, the same tab hangs from the middle of the screen's top edge with the chevron turned down. Neither shows text. Each shows its name as a tooltip on hover and on keyboard focus.

1. Bar open

The tab straddles the bottom border. Chevron up means hide. The tooltip is forced open here so it can be read; in the app it appears on hover or focus.

2. Bar hidden

Only the 8 px strip is left, with the tab hanging from it. Chevron down means show. Nothing in the page moves when the bar returns except the content below it, as today.

3. Hidden, connection lost

The strip turns red and the alert banner stays, exactly as now. The tab keeps its place and its neutral color, so the alert is not hidden under it.

4. Hidden, in Wall mode

Wall mode's own buttons stay at the top right (out of scope for #402). The tab is in the middle; the two never meet.

5. At 400 px

The bar wraps to two rows. The tab is out of the flow, so it never covers a button, and it stays on the bottom border of the last row.

Rules shown here

  • Both controls are real buttons with no text. Each keeps its aria-label (Hide top bar, Show top bar), aria-expanded and aria-controls="podium-top", is a Tab stop, and shows a visible focus ring and its tooltip on keyboard focus too.
  • The \ shortcut and the remembered collapsed state are unchanged. The tooltip carries the name only, as the issue asks.
  • The tab is absolutely placed at 50% of the bar, so the logo, page pill, connection pill and the right-hand buttons cannot collide with it and "synced Xs ago" ticking cannot move it.
  • The chevron is an icon, not a color: the open and hidden states differ by its direction, the tooltip and aria-expanded. No new color token is used (--raised, --border, --sel, --ink, --ink2).
  • When the bar is hidden the tab is transparent and always shown. The old strip button was invisible until hover; this one never fades, so it can be found without hunting for it.
  • The hidden strip grows from 4 px to 8 px so the tab hangs from a visible edge. That is the only existing size that changes.
  • The wall-notice rules are not touched. Wall mode, the lost-connection alert with the bar collapsed and the left and right pane controls keep working as they do now.
  • This is a new small component (an icon tab). If the maintainer accepts it, it is added to the standards in the release-prep PR with a Standards: line, not in the feature PR.

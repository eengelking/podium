Fold the display controls into a Wall options drawer

Refresh every, Shortcuts, Theme and Light / Dark / System are set once and rarely touched, but today they fill the right of the banner on every visit, and six pages each draw their own copy. This folds them into one shared drawer, WallOptions, that every page renders the same way.

Try it

Shut, the right of the banner shows one pill. Hover or focus it to read "Wall options". Click it to open the drawer: the pill moves to the drawer's left end and turns into its collapse button, and the four controls follow in today's order. Close it with that button, a click anywhere outside the drawer, or Escape, which puts focus back on the pill.

The same on every page

These are the six banners that show the row today, each with the one component. They share one setting store, as the pages do through localStorage: set Refresh every on Admin, then open Live fleet, and it shows the new value. History and Admin show and save Refresh every and Shortcuts even though they do not act on them yet.

How it behaves

WhatHow
StartShut on every page load. Whether it was open is not stored.
OpenClick the pill, or Enter or Space on it. Focus stays on the pill, which is now at the drawer's left end; Tab goes on through Refresh every, Shortcuts, Theme and the mode radios, then out of the banner.
CloseThe same pill (now the collapse button); a press anywhere outside the drawer; Escape, from anywhere on the page while it is open, which puts focus back on the pill.
Stays openAny click inside the drawer: a select, picking a theme from it, the Shortcuts switch, a mode. Opening the Keyboard shortcuts list from the word Shortcuts keeps the drawer open behind it, and Escape closes the list first.
NamesThe pill is a button with aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at the controls. Its name says what a click does, as every EdgeTab's does: "Wall options" shut, "Hide wall options" open. The tip carries the same words.
SettingsUnchanged: podium.updateEvery, podium.shortcuts and the theme and mode keys. Each page passes what it has (the live interval on the console pages); History and Admin read and write the stored value directly.
MotionThe controls slide 8 px in from the right over .15s as the drawer opens, and not at all with reduced motion. Where the text and the drawer fit on one row, shut and open have the same height, so the wall below does not move.

Standards change

Collapse and open controls are EdgeTab (components.html#top-bar, #626). This keeps that rule and widens EdgeTab by one placement rather than adding a new kind of button.

Proposed
  1. EdgeTab gains a placement, opts. Same 32 by 24 pill, border, radius, hover and tip as the others. It sits in the row at the right end of the banner instead of on an edge, and its tip lines up with the side it is on. Shut it draws a sliders glyph, the one EdgeTab that does not draw a chevron, because what it opens has no edge to point at. Open it draws the chevron pointing right, the way the drawer folds.
  2. A new component, Wall options (components/WallOptions.tsx, .wallopts), in the Top bar section: the pill plus the four controls, the only way a page shows them. It replaces .bannerctl, which goes away.
  3. One pill for open and close. The issue asks for an icon that opens the drawer and a collapse button at the drawer's left end. Here they are the same button: it moves from the right end to the left end as the drawer opens. That keeps focus where it was, keeps aria-expanded on a button that is always there, and matches how the top bar's and the panes' EdgeTabs work.

If you would rather keep EdgeTab to chevrons only, the other way is a separate icon button (a new component) that hides while the drawer is open, with a chevron EdgeTab as the collapse button inside it. It costs one more component, two buttons for one action, and focus that has to be moved by hand on open.

For review

  • Accept the one-pill design (EdgeTab opts with the sliders glyph), or ask for the separate icon button.
  • The name and tip: "Wall options" shut and "Hide wall options" open.
  • The drawer on --surface with a --border outline inside the --raised banner.

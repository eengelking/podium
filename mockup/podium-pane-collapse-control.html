Pane collapse control

Issue #626. The left and right panes' edge buttons become the pill the top bar, the Kanban KPI rows and the folded Plan / Files column already use: bordered, 10 px chevron, a styled tip on hover and keyboard focus, the Sonnet focus ring. The chevron points the way the pane will move. The tip keeps the shortcut ("Collapse the left pane ([)"). Names, aria-expanded, aria-controls, the shortcuts, the saved state and wall mode are unchanged.

What exists today

Left: the pane button being replaced (24 x 26, native tooltip, hover fill only). The others are the controls it should match: the top bar's pill, the KPI rows' pill and the Plan / Files fold, which is already the 16 x 32 vertical shape.

Option A: pill near the top of the edge, in both states (recommended)

14 px below the top, where .fold-tab sits, so it stays clear of the middle of the resize handle. Open, the pill straddles the pane's inner edge. Collapsed, it sits in the 24 px strip the pane leaves behind with the chevron turned, so a collapsed pane is reachable at the edge it left. The tip opens toward the page.

Option B: pill centered on the edge, the collapsed strip is the button

Open, the pill is centered along the edge, over the resize handle's midpoint. Collapsed, the whole 24 px strip is the button and hover fills it: the biggest target of the two.

Narrow (400 px, the panes stack)

At 1040 px and below the handles are gone and the panes stack: rail, page, activity. The pill becomes a 28 px full-width bar, the size of the .pane-tab it replaces and bigger than the 16 px pill. It closes the rail below it and opens the activity panel above it; chevrons point up and down. Same border, hover, focus ring and tip (opening upward on the activity bar).

Respond to a waiting session from Podium

When a session stops to ask something, the amber band gets a Respond button. It opens the Activity modal, where you can answer the question, approve or reject a plan, allow or deny a tool, or send a new message or /clear. The answer goes to the session through podium-reporter, and only after you sign in with your name and token (section 6). The terminal or desktop app still works the whole time, and whichever answers first wins. Section 3 shows the modal in every case. To click through one yourself, pick a kind of wait and a state, then press Respond on the card.

1. The card on the wall

The button sits where you circled it, at the right end of the band. The count says how many questions are open in one ask (Claude Code sends up to four at a time). In the states where Podium can't send anything, the button is gone or dimmed with the reason beside it, and the band reads as it does today.

Rules for the button

  • Shown only while the session waits on you, and only when every switch in section 5 is on.
  • One name in every case: Respond. The modal says what kind of answer is needed.
  • Signed out, it is shown on every waiting card and leads to signing in (section 6).
  • Not on a card that waits on background work (blue band), since nothing there needs you.
  • The card's own click target is unchanged: it still opens the orchestrator page. Respond opens the modal straight from the wall.

2. The orchestrator page

The same band, and the same button, is the last row of the summary at the top of the page. The Expand button on the orchestrator's Activity panel opens the same modal, so you can send a message even while the session is still running (it waits in a queue until the session is idle).

3. The modal, case by case

Each frame is the modal as it opens from Respond in that case, with the feed cut to its last few rows. The page behind it is blurred in the real thing. To click through one, use the controls at the top and press Respond on the card.

When the server refuses an answer for any other reason, the message box shows one line instead of the server's own words: "This session doesn't take that from Podium now. Answer it in the terminal." (a kind the session no longer takes), "20 messages are already waiting for this session. Send more once it has caught up.", "Podium won't take this from you: it isn't your session, or responding was turned off.", or "Podium couldn't send that. Try again."

4. What the terminal shows

podium-reporter writes one line above the prompt while the question is also open in Podium, and one line in the transcript when it was answered from there, naming who answered. Someone at the terminal is never surprised by an answer they didn't give. A Deny sent with no reason reaches Claude as "Denied from Podium.", and No, keep planning with nothing typed as "No, keep planning."

While the question is open. The terminal dialog works as usual.

After an answer from Podium. The dialog closes by itself.

The other answers from Podium: a Deny, a plan sent back, and a message in place of an answer.

While responding is on and the session is idle, podium-reporter writes messages from Podium are on. once, the first time the session is idle with messages from Podium allowed. It writes it again after /clear, which empties the transcript, and after responding was turned off and back on.

5. The switches

Off by default, and every one is needed. The Admin decides whether this server may send anything; the person running the session decides how much their session accepts, and can always say no. Answering also needs the activity feed, which already has both kinds of switch.

Respond from Podium Admin > Settings > Feature flags. Lets people answer and message the sessions they own. Each answer is written to the admin log with who sent it.

In Claude Code's settings the option's description is this list on one line, each level followed by its text.

6. Signing in

Anyone can watch, but answering needs Podium to know who you are. You sign in with the same name and token your podium-reporter uses, from either of two places: Respond, when you're signed out (section 3, "Signing in"), or Sign in at the far right of the top bar, past a divider after History, Hide top bar and Admin. Signed in, that spot shows your name and Sign out.

Rules for signing in

  • Name is your name as Admin > People shows it, the same as the reporter's userName option. Upper or lower case doesn't matter.
  • Token is your own token, the reporter's token option. Someone in Administrators signs in the same way and can respond to every session. The admin secret signs in with the name Admin.
  • One sign-in per browser, shared by its tabs. It ends after 12 hours without use, on Sign out, when the server restarts, and when your token is revoked or rotated.
  • The page never keeps your token. Five wrong tries from one address lock signing in for 15 minutes, counted together with wrong Admin unlocks.
  • Signed out, every card still shows Respond, because Podium doesn't know which sessions are yours. Signed in, other people's sessions show Respond dimmed, with their name.
  • Sign in and Sign out stay at the far right of the top bar, after the divider, so the buttons before it don't move when you sign in or out.

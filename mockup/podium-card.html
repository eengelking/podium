Podium card redesign

Every card gets the same four layers in the same order. Who it is, what it is doing, what it costs, then one status band that sits at the bottom edge of every card, whatever the card holds.

The problem, and the fix

Today the colored bar sits wherever the content above it ends. Beside each other, three waiting cards put it at three heights. In the proposal it is always the last thing in the card, so cards in a row end on the same line and the eye goes to one place.

Today

The bar floats. A plan, an agent strip or a longer path moves it.

Proposed

The band is pinned to the bottom edge, full width, same height on every card.

Anatomy of one card

Read top to bottom: who, what it is doing, what it costs and how healthy it is, then the verdict. Dividers between layers do the grouping that the single crowded column does not.

Who is this, and where?Name, model and effort. Below it the folder, the branch and whether the tree is clean. Notes such as “nobody attached” or a reporter warning sit last in this layer, and only appear when they apply.
What is it doing?The plan step and its progress, then the agents it launched and how busy the session is right now (tokens per second). Without a plan this layer shrinks to one quiet line.
What is it costing, and how full is it?Four readouts in fixed columns: cost, tokens, time and context. A blowout count joins the context column and turns the card’s top edge red.
Does it need me?The status band. Same position, same height, same size icon on every card. Calm states (running, done, background wait) are a light tint. Waiting and heartbeat lost are a heavier tint. Every band has the same thin top line.

Decisions to confirm

These come from your notes and from the layout above. Say yes, or change any of them.

  • The band bleeds to the card edge.Content keeps its 12 px padding. Only the band runs edge to edge, which is what makes it read as a base.
  • The status chip leaves the header.The band carries the words. A small shape beside the name still marks the state when a card is skimmed.
  • The blowout sentence leaves the card.The red top edge, the count and the red context figure stay. The sentence stays in the hover text and on the orchestrator page, so no fact is lost.
  • The sparkline moves up beside the agents.Tokens per second says how busy the session is, so it sits with “what it is doing”, not with the totals.
  • Reporter notes become small tags.“settings hooks” and “reporter 0.2.1 · update” shrink to tags in the first layer, shown only when they apply. Hooks-only sessions show a dash where cost and context cannot be known.
  • Side panel: no change needed.One suggestion: the plan’s full step list and the blowout sentence could both open from the card with one click on the band. Say if you want that.

Your call. Approve this, or tell me what to move. Nothing gets built until you do.

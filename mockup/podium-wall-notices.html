The rule for every wall notice, now and later

  1. It sits at the bottom of the wall, below the cards, and never above or between them.
  2. It has an X. Closing it turns that notice off and shows the confirmation that points to Admin > Settings.
  3. It has a switch in Admin > Settings > Wall notices, with a one-line description of what it says.
  4. If the notice has an action (Show them, Turn cache alerts back on), the action stays as a link in the notice.
  5. Errors and connection problems are not notices: they stay where they are and cannot be closed.

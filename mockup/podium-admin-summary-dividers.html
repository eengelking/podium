What changes

Every .sum row on the Admin pages becomes a bordered grid of cells with a 1 px divider, built the way .podium .kpis is on the wall (1 px gap over var(--border), var(--surface) cells). Nothing new is drawn: no new color, no new component.

Scope

All 16 div.sum rows (every Admin > Reporting page, Spend and its projection, Stored activity, Harness). Other div.tool rows hold controls, not figures, and are left as they are, apart from the report-version counts (.cuts) which I will check when I build it.

Standard

This extends the KPI row's look to Admin. If you accept it, a standards fragment names the KPI row as also used on Admin summary rows.

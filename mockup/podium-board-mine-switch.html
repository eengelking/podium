Board: a "mine" switch beside the layout controls

Issue #451. The Board's Me chip works today but is easy to miss: one small chip after the Group dropdown, shown only to a signed-in viewer. This mockup turns it into a two-button switch in the Board's own segmented control, the same shape as Plans | All and Mixed | By orchestrator. Behavior is unchanged: it narrows the cards only, the tiles stay whole fleet, and the choice is kept in podium.board. Try it in both modes, all three wordings, and signed in or out.

Proposed: wide

Press "My orchestrators" and the cards below keep only the signed-in person's sessions. The four tiles do not change and read "whole fleet" as they do today with Me.

Wording (recommend "My orchestrators | Everyone")Your "My orchestrators | All" reads well, but the Board header already has a Plans | All switch, and two buttons both named All next to each other are hard to tell apart for a screen reader user and for a glance. "Everyone" avoids that. "Mine | Everyone" is shorter if the header gets crowded. Pick one with the Wording buttons above.
Placement (recommend after the Group dropdown)It takes the Me chip's place at the right end, after Mixed | By orchestrator and All groups, so the three controls that narrow or arrange the cards sit together. The Plans | All switch stays by the title.
Not signed in (recommend hide, as today)The Fleet page's Me is hidden for a viewer nobody has signed in, and the switch follows it. A disabled switch with a hint would need a place to explain how to sign in, and the Board has none. Say if you would rather show it disabled.
What is the sameTwo real buttons with aria-pressed, the group named "Whose sessions" for screen readers, the same focus ring, Tab and Enter or Space. No new component. A remembered choice with nobody signed in falls back to everyone, as the Me chip does.

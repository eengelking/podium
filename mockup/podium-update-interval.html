Refresh every (#138)

One new "Refresh every" select in the fleet banner, to the left of the Theme controls. It sets how often the console redraws from new data and moves its clock. The pulse and blink beat is unchanged. Fleet page only; the choice is kept per browser.

In the fleet banner (default, 2 s)

Podium
Live fleet. Cost is an estimate from token counts and a price table, not a bill.
Cards, KPIs, side panels and elapsed times redraw every 2s.

Choices

2s5s10s20s30s60s

At 60s

Live fleet. Cost is an estimate from token counts and a price table, not a bill.
Anything that needs you (a session waiting, a failure, a lost connection) still shows at once.

What redraws when

ChangeShows
New tokens, cost, context, agents, activityOn the next update
Elapsed times and the clockOn the next update
A session starts waiting for you, or failsAt once
The connection to the server drops or returnsAt once
Pulse ring and blinksUnchanged (shared beat)

Decided: the label and the choices (2s to 60s) match the Activity feed's own "Refresh every" control.

Decisions this mockup proposes (to be written into SPEC 8.1)

Stored per browserIn localStorage under podium.pins, with try/catch, like the other wall choices. Not per person: no server change.
A pin is a slotIt holds the card's place in reading order (1st, 2nd, 3rd...) within its wall or band, taken when pinned. Two pins never share a slot.
ColumnsThe slot is a place in the flow, so with more or fewer columns it still reads left to right, top to bottom. It is not a row and column.
Filter, Group by, Only meA filtered-out pinned card is hidden and the others close up, with no gap; it returns to its slot when it matches again. Under Group by a pin holds its slot inside its band. A pin hidden by Only me is kept.
Departed sessionsA pin for a session that ended or was removed is dropped. A session that is only hidden keeps its pin.
Slot past the endWith fewer cards than the slot number, the card goes last and returns to its slot when more cards arrive.

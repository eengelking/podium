Edge tab: a 24 px target

Issue #636. WCAG 2.5.8 (Target Size, Minimum) wants a pointer target at least 24 by 24 CSS px, or an undersized one with no other target inside a 24 px circle around it. The edge tab is 32 x 16 on the top bar and the KPI rows, and 16 x 32 on the left and right panes and the Plan / Files fold, so its short side is 16. axe reports target-size on the wall, Board, My reporters and Traffic. It is likely the neighbouring targets (the pane's resize handle, a control just under the bar, the next column) that make the spacing exception fail, which is why the options below are judged on both size and what the bigger box touches.

Every placement is drawn four ways from the same markup: today's tab, and three options that reach at least 24 x 24. Under each is the box the browser measures (the number axe reads) and which neighbours it overlaps. Names, aria-expanded, aria-controls, chevrons, tips, shortcuts and the focus ring are unchanged in every option.

The options

Today: 32 x 16 pill
  • Under 24 on its short side in every placement. Fails.
  • Straddles the resize handle (panes), the bar's edge (top) and the gap (fold).
A (recommended): larger visible pill, 32 x 24 and 24 x 32
  • Pro: the simplest change, one rule per placement, and the visible target and the real target are the same box, so a person sees what they can hit.
  • Pro: nothing hidden to explain or test.
  • Con: 8 px thicker everywhere; the visible weight on the pane edges and the top bar goes up by half.
  • Con: straddles 12 px, not 8, so it reaches further into what sits beside the edge: about 4 px more into a control under the bar. The resize handle is no worse off: today's tab already covers its whole 9 px width, and the tab's length along the handle does not change.
B: same visible pill, a transparent 24 px box around it
  • Pro: nothing changes visually; every layout stays exactly as it is.
  • Pro: the button element itself is 24 high, so axe measures 24 (a hit area drawn only by a pseudo-element would not be measured).
  • Con: the extra 4 px each side is invisible and sits on top of the neighbour: it reaches into any control within 4 px (a banner control under the top bar), and that overlap cannot be seen.
  • Con: the focus ring and hover fill move to a pseudo-element, so they no longer follow the box.
C: a 24 x 24 disc
  • Pro: smallest footprint that meets the minimum: it is 24 in both directions, so it is no wider than A's short side and it needs no rotating for the vertical edges.
  • Pro: one shape in every placement; folded bars keep a 24 px bar.
  • Con: it is a new look (the pill is the identity #626 chose); a standards change is larger.
  • Con: the chevron stays 10 px in a bigger disc, which reads lighter.

Every placement, four ways

Left to right: today, A, B, C. At 400 px they stack one above the other. The last two rows are the stacked layouts the Plan / Files fold takes below 1040 px, where the pill turns 90 degrees and, folded, becomes a bar across the width; the left and right panes are already 28 px bars at that width.
Recommendation: ATake A, the larger visible pill (32 x 24 and 24 x 32). It is the only option where what a person sees is what they can hit, it needs no pseudo-element trick, and it is a size change to a documented component rather than a new component. If the maintainer finds the 8 px extra thickness too heavy on the pane edges, B is the fallback: it keeps today's look and still measures 24, at the cost of an invisible 4 px reach into whatever sits next to the edge. All four variants, today's included, already sit on the pane's resize handle (the handle is a target the tab fully covers); no option fixes that, and the code PR should decide whether the handle should end short of the tab. C is the option to pick only if a new shape is wanted anyway.
What would change in codeOnly .edge-tab and its placement offsets in fleet.css (size, the straddle offset, the folded rail's tab position, the folded bars' height). EdgeTab.tsx and its markup do not change. The offsets move from 8 to 12 px to keep the pill centred on its edge.

Tokens

Every color and font token, named by its role. They live in frontend/src/fleet/tokens.css and nowhere else: the app, these pages and new mockups all read that file. The swatches follow the theme and mode picked above. The values shown are Podium's own, dark and light.

Surfaces

TokenRolePodium darkPodium light
--bgThe page behind everything, and the text color on a primary button.#0d0d0d#f9f9f7
--surfaceCards, panels, the top bar and the KPI strip: anything that sits on the page.#1a1a19#fcfcfb
--raisedSomething that sits on a surface or floats above it: buttons, tooltips, the toast, the modal's raised parts.#222220#f1f0ec

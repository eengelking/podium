Allow reporters to use the admin secret

Reporters that send the admin secret show in the Admin band. Anyone who has this secret can also open Admin, so keep it to your own machine and give everyone else their own token.

On

What protects this

  • Tokens are stored hashed. Podium cannot show one again.
  • Podium decides who a report is from by its token, not by the name in it.
  • Revoking takes effect on the next report.
  • Wrong guesses at the admin secret are limited and logged in the Admin log.
  • Tokens travel as they do today. On a network, use a trusted network or a TLS proxy.
